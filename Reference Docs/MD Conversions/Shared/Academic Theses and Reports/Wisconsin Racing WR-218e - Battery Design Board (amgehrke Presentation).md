## UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN - MADISION

## Battery Pack Specs

- Nominal Voltage: 216 VDC
- Capacity: 31 Amp-hours Capacity: 31 Amp-hours
- Energy: 6.70 Kilowatt-hour Energy: 6.70 Kilowatt-hour
- Built from 120 cell modules (60s2p) Built from 120 cell modules (60s2p)
- Each module is 1s5p and has integrated temperature sensors and cell level fuses Each module is 1s5p and has integrated temperature sensors and cell level fuses
- Student designed Battery Management System Priority Power Multiplexer for seamless switchover from LV Batt to the DCDC Student designed Battery Management System Priority Power Multiplexer for seamless switchover from LV Batt to the DCDC
- Onboard diagnostics MCU that measure all shutdown circuit signals

## Battery Management System

- Cortex-M4 MCU with the LTC6811 BMS IC
- Monitors cell voltages, cell temperatures and pack current
- Makes sure the cells are within their safe operating limits
- Reports Troubleshooting codes to quickly troubleshoot fault scenarios Battery Management System Dataflow
